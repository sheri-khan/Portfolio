About Me

Hi, I'm Sheri. I'm an AI Engineer who works with AI — integrating models, building computer vision systems, and using AI-assisted development to ship real products. I don't train foundation models from scratch; I make AI useful inside practical applications.

On the creative side, I'm a video editor and content creator. I run two YouTube channels — SherixEditz (dynamic cricket edits) and The Missing Minute (quick, insightful tutorials) — where pacing, motion, and storytelling matter as much as the code.

I also work across the full stack: React, Node, Flutter, cloud deployments, and databases — so AI features actually land in production, not just in notebooks.
